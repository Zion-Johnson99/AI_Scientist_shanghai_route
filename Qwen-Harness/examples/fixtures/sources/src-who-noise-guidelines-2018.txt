Key statements:
- The guideline recommends reducing noise levels produced by road traffic to below
  Lden 53 dB, as road traffic noise above this level is associated with adverse
  health effects (annoyance and sleep disturbance).
- For railway traffic the recommended level is Lden 54 dB; for aircraft noise the
  recommendation is Lden 45 dB (night levels lower).
- The guidelines are based on systematic reviews of exposure-effect evidence and
  use health-outcome-specific recommendations rather than a single global limit.
