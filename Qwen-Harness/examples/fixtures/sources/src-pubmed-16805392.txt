PMID: 16805392
Title: Health effects of fine particulate air pollution: lines that connect.
Authors: Pope CA 3rd, Dockery DW.
Journal: Journal of the Air & Waste Management Association, 2006.
DOI: 10.1080/10473289.2006.10464485

Abstract (abridged, fixture):
Critical evaluation of the scientific literature suggests that fine particulate
air pollution (PM2.5) is associated with increased cardiopulmonary mortality.
Long-term cohort studies report meaningful associations between chronic fine
particulate exposure and total, cardiovascular, and lung-cancer mortality.
The evidence supports a causal interpretation for PM2.5 exposure and
cardiopulmonary health outcomes, with effect estimates increasing roughly in
proportion to long-term mean concentrations observed across cohorts.
