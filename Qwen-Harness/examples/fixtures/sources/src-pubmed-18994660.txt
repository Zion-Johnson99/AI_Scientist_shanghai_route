PMID: 18994660
Title: Effect of exposure to natural environment on health inequalities: an
observational population study.
Authors: Mitchell R, Popham F.
Journal: Lancet (London, England), 2008.
DOI: 10.1016/S0140-6736(08)61689-X

Abstract (abridged, fixture):
This observational population study examined whether access to greenspace could
explain variation in health inequalities by income. Populations with the highest
exposure to greenspace had the lowest health inequalities, and exposure to
greenspace was associated with reduced all-cause mortality. The findings suggest
that environments promoting physical activity, such as natural or green
environments, may confer health benefits and narrow health gaps.
