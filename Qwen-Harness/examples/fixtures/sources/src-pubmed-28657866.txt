PMID: 28657866
Title: Air Pollution and Mortality in the Medicare Population.
Authors: Di Q, Wang Y, Zanobetti A, Wang Y, Koutrakis P, Choirat C, Dominici F,
Schwartz JD.
Journal: The New England Journal of Medicine, 2017.
DOI: 10.1056/NEJMoa1702747

Abstract (abridged, fixture):
In a nationwide cohort of Medicare enrollees (65 years of age or older),
long-term exposure to fine particulate matter (PM2.5) and ozone was associated
with higher risk of death from any cause. The association with PM2.5 persisted
at annual concentrations below the current U.S. national ambient air quality
standard of 12 ug per cubic metre, indicating that risk increases even at low
concentrations. Effect estimates were robust across model specifications.
