Key guideline values for outdoor ambient air quality:
- Fine particulate matter (PM2.5): annual mean guideline value 5 micrograms per
  cubic metre (ug/m3); 24-hour peak season guideline value 15 ug/m3.
- The guidelines are evidence-based recommendations derived from systematic
  review of the epidemiological literature on health effects of air pollution.
- Evidence for long-term exposure effects on all-cause and cardiopulmonary
  mortality is rated as high certainty for PM2.5.
